# Failed It photos: sources and credits

Real photos of other people's bad installs for the Failed It side of the
Monday card (Henry 2026-11-07). Never a Livewire crew's work.

- Source: Wikimedia Commons, openly licensed only (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA).
- Each image is cropped to 4:3 (960x720) and carries the credit along its
  bottom edge, as the license asks. CC BY-SA images stay under their
  original license.
- These files go in the Drive folder *Failed It*
  (`1befbCvOuzb4bFO9u3sBzVmpxsaW0Lk8W`). The email rotates one per week,
  sorted by file name.
